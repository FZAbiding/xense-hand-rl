# Wuji v9 short-turn pilot: completed A/B screen

**2026-10-19. Machine fixed-seed result only; human motion review pending.** Both isolated arms finished their 20-minute effective training budgets without supervisor restarts. A completed 914 PPO updates and 29,949,952 environment steps; B completed 953 updates and 31,227,904 steps. Both began from the same v8 iteration 996 diagnostic weight and used the [pre-frozen local pad map](../../../../assets/wuji_hand2_palm_up_human_demo_v9/pad_regions.json). B alone added a 2-unit per-frame reward penalty when non-pad pen force exceeded 0.05 N. The 5.5-second task requests one signed 360° rotation during 3 seconds followed by regrasp.

## Limits and next gate

- The unseen seed `2026101703` remains unopened. Per the [frozen pilot contract](../../../../experiments/wuji_hand2/palm_up_human_demo_v9/PLAN.md), it will be evaluated only after human review accepts A900's contact placement, index/middle relationship, release and handover, and final regrasp. A single 5.5-second simulated turn is not evidence for continuous 30/60/120-second rotation.
- The first-episode videos are actual CPU policy rollouts with three synchronized camera views. The copied renderer mistakenly prints `V8` in the corner; the on-frame task name, checkpoint hashes, versioned assets, and raw metrics identify these as v9 rollouts. No physical frames were altered. The videos show one episode, while the linked metrics include all 24. B900's first-episode video may be a passing episode; its failures are documented by episode in its JSON.
- The pad classifier covers the exact user-accepted distal examples with 4 mm body-local neighborhoods plus the existing sensor collision bodies. This is a bounded engineering assumption, not an anatomical annotation of every distal surface. Occluded contacts and naturalness still need human judgement.
- B800's model was deleted by the inherited retention policy after its validation finished. Its metrics and video are preserved as historical evidence, but its reported 23/24 cannot be rerun from that checkpoint. The retained B953 scored 22/24 and B900 scored 20/24; B has no preserved model meeting the 23/24 fixed gate. A900 and A914 both have matching saved weights.
