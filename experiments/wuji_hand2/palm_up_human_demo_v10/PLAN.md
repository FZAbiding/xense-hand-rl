In v9's only curriculum level, the previous `level >= 2` condition made this mechanism inactive. No observation or reward change is made. A read-only preflight measured A900 at **24/24** under ±0.02 N and ±0.08 N pushes, then **18/24** under ±0.2 N pushes on the declared diagnostic seed. Thus ±0.2 N is the first tested challenge with failures; it is frozen before v10 training. This is an intentionally strong stress test on the 0.025 kg pen, not a claim about everyday handling.

## Frozen evaluation and selection

- Every 250 updates, run independent CPU MuJoCo validation for 24 episodes without disturbance on fixed seed `2026101701`, then 24 episodes with x/y pen pushes uniformly sampled within ±0.2 N on fixed **diagnostic** seed `2026102001`; pulse duration and period are 0.1 s and 2 s. The new diagnostic seed is a validation set, not the sealed unseen seed `2026101703`.
- Keep the v9 strict episode gate unchanged: complete 5.5 s; drift <0.025 m; MAE ≤10°; no index/middle posture or reverse bending violation; maximum active self-depth <0.0005 m; two calibrated pad fingers >95% of frames; non-pad force >0.05 N in <5% of frames; inactive force >0.02 N in <1%; opposing contact >90%; command clipping <5%; middle PIP excursion ≤35°; signed rotation progress 0.9–1.15; at least three stable pair handovers; terminal regrasp >90% and present at the last frame.
- Screening candidate: at least 23/24 standard strict passes and at least 20/24 pushed strict passes. Among candidates, select by pushed pass count, then standard pass count, then fewer non-pad failures, then earlier iteration. These are machine diagnostics pending human review. If none qualify, retain the preserved v9 A900 as the preferred candidate.
- Stop early after two consecutive paired validations with standard success below 20/24, at least two posture failures, or at least four incomplete episodes. Preserve all such failures. The selected and final models, all per-episode gate failures, uncut first-episode three-view videos, frame/force traces, and source hashes must be archived and pushed.

After the user's motion review, an acceptable candidate may be evaluated on the still sealed `2026101703` seed. Only a pass on fixed and unseen sets, plus favorable human review, can authorize a separate duration-bridge plan. No duration escalation is part of this overnight run.
